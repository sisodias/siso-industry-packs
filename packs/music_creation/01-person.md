# Stage 1 — The person: a beatmaker who needs to reopen and hand over the right project

Research date: **2026-09-13**. Evidence classes: `[vendor]` upstream product documentation or vendor reply; `[practitioner]` an attributed first-person forum report; `[analysis]` a proposed design or inference. Historical reports are dated below, not presented as current reproducible defects.

## What is actually known

The [existing project brief](README.md) identifies a low-priority consumer favour for a friend who makes beats and wants AI assistance. It explicitly says the original specification was lost and that this is the creator/artist workflow, **not professional editing-for-hire**. No interview, device inventory, bank statement, private conversation or original specification was recovered in this research pass. His DAW, operating system, plugin collection, earnings, collaborators and actual weekly workload therefore remain **unknown**. Public forum participants are not the client. [analysis; source: existing project brief]

## One primary operator, without pretending to have interviewed him

**Proposed research subject:** one independent, home/laptop-based beatmaker who starts instrumental ideas, revises them over several sessions, and occasionally exchanges a project or rendered stems with a collaborator. He owns his creative decisions and files; there is no assumed employer, studio staff, payroll or paying client. This primary segment is selected because project recovery, dependencies and handoff problems recur in the source record below and can be investigated without replacing musical judgement. It is a hypothesis to validate, not a newly recovered personal specification. [analysis; evidence: Q01–Q08 below]

**What the listener or collaborator receives:** a playable instrumental and, when requested, an agreed set of correctly named stems with the relevant version and credits. Whether anyone pays for those deliverables is unmeasured. The creative outcome is the music; file hunting, reconstructing plugin state, export checking and chasing the correct version are potential overhead. Do not invent beat-sale revenue to make a consumer favour look commercially qualified. [analysis]

**Selection boundary:** preserve the operator's current DAW until its identity is known. Do not assume Ableton, FL Studio, Logic or Cubase merely because a forum example uses it. Start with a read-only companion to existing projects; replacement-DAW work needs a separate case. [analysis]

## A test Tuesday — not an observed diary

The times are a proposed interview/replay scenario. They describe one coherent workflow, not claimed hours worked by the client or a productivity baseline. [analysis]

| Time | Concrete activity and persistent artifact | Where the work can stall | Evidence or status |
|---|---|---|---|

## Eight practitioner complaints — with replies and limits preserved

Each quotation is a short excerpt from a distinct linked discussion. Reading includes the surrounding exchange, including fixes and disagreement; no quote proves prevalence. **Seven sources are Steinberg forums and one is Xfer's forum.** This is a DAW/export-heavy convenience sample, not representative research on all beatmakers, genres, countries, release channels or willingness to pay. [analysis]

### Q01 — Same preset name, different sound across computers

> “Serum 2 loads with the correct preset name, but the sound resets to the default "Init" patch.”

**hughntra, 2025-06-01**, [Collect All Save & Serum 2](https://xferrecords.com/forums/general/collect-all-save-serum-2). [practitioner]

The author reports Ableton/Serum projects moving between a PC and Mac using OneDrive and Collect All and Save. **The vendor replied on 2025-09-25 that the receiving Serum version was older than the saved session's version; newer sessions are not forward-compatible.** This is evidence for recording plugin versions and validating reopen behaviour, not proof that every Serum project is broken or that copying more samples fixes it. [vendor; same source]

### Q02 — Comprehensive settings backups are cumbersome

> “it is a rather cumbersome task to do more comprehensive backups and restores manually.”

**ASM, 2026-01-09**, [Backup & Restore Scripts for Cubase on Windows](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/backup-restore-scripts-for-cubase-on-windows/1020325). [practitioner]

The author supplied a community workaround. Its stated scope excludes project audio, installed third-party plugins and licence activations; it focuses on settings/presets. **An existing workaround is part of the solution landscape**, not evidence for rebuilding it. A settings backup and a recoverable musical project are different deliverables. [practitioner; analysis]

### Q03 — A lower edition turns a batch operation into repeated manual work

> “I’m finding more and more functions I would consider basic to be missing from Elements.”

**Peter_Chackowsky, 2025-02-16**, [Audio Mixdown to Stems](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/audio-mixdown-to-stems/972981). [practitioner]

The discussion distinguishes manual per-track export from Pro's multichannel batch export. This is a historical edition-specific complaint; it does not establish the feature matrix of an unidentified current client installation. Recheck the actual edition before pricing an upgrade or claiming a missing feature. [practitioner; analysis]

### Q04 — A dry export is not necessarily the requested channel layout

> “I dont want that, I want a mono file like it as created.”

**Marbles, 2025-07-02**, [Exporting Selected Events](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/exporting-selected-events/998265). [practitioner]

A reply explains stereo-bus routing, and the author confirms on **2025-07-09** that the advice worked. This was **resolved configuration confusion**, not evidence that Cubase lacks mono export. The potential companion feature is a clear delivery check against requested channels, not a replacement renderer. [practitioner; analysis]

### Q05 — Turning an idea into a properly placed project takes extra steps

> “I wanted to save it off to a new folder - Backup Project is greyed out.”

**The_Elf, 2026-05-30**, [BACKUP PROJECT FUNCTION GONE.. kind of](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/backup-project-function-gone-kind-of/979208). [practitioner]

The original March 2025 discussion concerns using Backup Project before a first save so recorded audio goes into the desired project folder. A save-first workaround is described by users. The May 2026 reply is a later recurrence in that thread, not an independently reproduced defect or a measured rate. [practitioner; analysis]

### Q06 — Export selection requires checking against the actual deliverables

> “I had to manually go through all channels to see what I got and what not.”

**martinz, 2025-05-23**, [Export Audio Mixdown Multiple Selection Checkbox Issue](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/export-audio-mixdown-multiple-selection-checkbox-issue/991748). [practitioner]

The report concerns a large project and disabled external-instrument tracks. One respondent reproduces a selection problem; another cannot. Retain that disagreement. The useful requirement is stable track identifiers and an expected-versus-produced manifest, not an assertion that the same bug affects a small beat project today. [practitioner; analysis]

### Q07 — Co-writing and remote recording are different jobs

> “We are not engaging in a recording project, but a writing/production project.”

**KYRRHOF, 2022-09-30**, [Project Collaboration](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/project-collaboration/809476). [practitioner; historical]

Replies discuss other collaboration options, including an upgrade-dependent route the author says is not always feasible. This older Nuendo/Cubase/Dorico example helps distinguish asynchronous asset exchange from remote recording. It is **not** a current inventory of available services and **not** proof the primary beatmaker needs real-time co-editing. [practitioner; analysis]

### Q08 — The backup specification is hidden in scattered folders

> “it seems unimaginably complex: noting all the the different files and their locations that need to be backed up.”

**dibraccio, 2025-06-20**, [Backing up Cubase Preferences](https://forums.steinberg.net/t/backing-up-cubase-preferences/996222). [practitioner]

Subsequent replies identify an additional MediaBay location and direct readers to community scripts. This records a practitioner-maintained workaround and a completeness question, not a certified backup procedure. A local companion should inventory dependencies transparently and avoid claiming to back up activations or everything on the machine. [practitioner; analysis]

## Eight workflow-distinct segments

These are proposed research segments, not measured market shares or discovered customer counts. [analysis]

| Segment | What makes the workflow different | Priority for this pack |
|---|---|---|
| **Independent beatmaker sharing revisions** | Repeated project reopen, sample/plugin dependencies, occasional stem delivery and feedback. | **Primary**: directly tests the proposed project-to-handoff hypothesis without replacing the DAW. |
| Solo songwriter releasing own songs | Vocal recording, lyrics, credits and distribution become more central. | Adjacent; preserve release extension. |
| Beat-store seller | Multiple licence tiers, checkout, delivery entitlements and buyer support. | Separate commercial workflow; do not attribute to the client. |
| Remote co-writing pair | Cross-DAW handoff, version reconciliation and contributor decisions. | Adjacent; no assumption of simultaneous editing. |
| Sample-pack creator | Batch editing, metadata, licence packaging and large asset libraries. | Different primary product: reusable samples. |
| Live electronic performer | Set preparation, hardware/routing stability and offline show recovery. | Different failure cost; exclude live-performance reliability claims. |
| Media/sync composer | Cue variants, brief revisions, timed delivery and rights administration. | Professional workflow; not the consumer favour's default. |
| AI-first experimental creator | Generation, model/weight permissions, provenance, selection and reproducibility. | Optional lane; never assume a generated clip is cleared for release. |

## What this evidence changes

**Working hypothesis:** help the operator identify, reopen and hand over the correct project version, with a dependency and provenance record. Native DAW collection/export features and community backup tools remain the baseline to beat. The benefit is not established by eight complaints, and the source sample does not yet show that this is the client's main pain. [analysis; Q01–Q08]

The minimum validation record is one voluntarily supplied project, its actual DAW/plugin versions, the operator's normal reopen-and-send procedure, the delivered files, and time including repair/review. Keep that material on the user's machine. Reject the wedge if native tools and a simple naming convention solve the problem just as well, or if maintaining the companion costs more effort than it saves. [analysis]

**Stage receipt:** 8 distinct quoted practitioner discussions; 8 proposed segments; 1 primary segment; 0 client interviews; 0 measured time-saving observations. Source breadth beyond DAW/project operations remains a limitation.
