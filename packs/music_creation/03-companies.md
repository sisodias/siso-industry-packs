## Two competitors directly challenge the proposed wedge

**Saltado Sessions:** the vendor advertises a local/no-account library, compatibility checking and plugin information; paid tools identify used samples and link recognized Splice files to their packs even after collection/renaming. Licence-certificate retrieval still belongs to Splice's own flow. Ableton receives the deepest parsing. The vendor also describes local backup/portable-copy workflows and says merging changed work back from a second machine is still being built. These are capability claims, not independently tested safety/restore guarantees. [vendor: https://www.saltadosessions.com/features and https://saltadosessions.com/features]

**Kora / Export Flow:** the vendor advertises a local-first, Mac-first companion that launches rather than replaces the DAW. Export Flow already combines rendering, naming/folders, embedded metadata, preflight and a delivery ledger. Its explicit limitation is that preflight **does not analyze the audio**, including loudness or true peak. That is a bounded product limitation, not proof that no competing tool covers those checks. Optional cloud/AI must be evaluated separately from offline project data. [vendor: https://www.soniteq.co/ and https://www.soniteq.co/export-flow]

**Consequence:** “a dashboard with projects, samples and export checks” is not a defensible unserved gap. A new build must beat native tools plus these companions on one accepted operator task, without increasing maintenance or changing the sound. A parser, manifest or local processing adapter may be more valuable than another whole application. [analysis]

### The unglamorous operating system

**Google Sheets / Excel:** no actual client spreadsheet was supplied. The proposed interview should look for one row per work/version, file path, BPM/key, collaborator, split status, release date, identifier, revenue statement and unpaid balance. These fields are **a proposed inspection checklist**, not an extracted sheet. A manually maintained sheet can remain the baseline; ownership, formulas, attachments and export/restore behaviour are what must be learned before replacing it. [analysis]

**Notion / Airtable / Monday / ClickUp:** Notion and Airtable were priced above; ThorneLabs is concrete music-specific template evidence. Monday/ClickUp were not profiled and are not counted. The repeated transformation from generic records into music catalogues suggests a record-layer opportunity, but copying a template is not proof of an unmet need. [vendor: https://thornelabs.io/; analysis]

**Accounting, payroll and payments:** QuickBooks is a business comparator, not an assumed subscription. No employee means no evidence of a payroll need. Stripe, Square, bank transfer and cash are possible payment routes to investigate only if paid work is confirmed; account fees, volumes and transaction rates remain unknown. An invoice/receipt can be referenced as a local document without connecting financial accounts. [analysis; accounting capability source O16]

**Calendar, paper, phone and memory:** actual usage is unknown. A simple checklist/calendar entry might outperform a new tool for a low-frequency creator. Paper has a purchase cost and human transcription burden even where there is no software subscription; do not equate a zero subscription with zero switching cost. [analysis]

## STACK TAX — show the arithmetic without pretending alternatives are compulsory

A sum of every product above would buy several mutually exclusive DAWs, duplicated administration, unpriced services and mixed currencies. It is not a coherent operator stack. **All-menu total = unknown**, and **actual client annual spend = unknown**. Below are explicit USD reference baskets for comparison, not recommendations, observed bills or cancellable savings. Amounts marked$ on Splice's displayed plan are treated as USD for these reference calculations; checkout currency must be confirmed before budgeting. No exchange-rate conversion is used. [analysis]

### A. Modest hypothetical release-capable basket

One eligible REAPER licence, regular-price Splice Creator for sound supply, and TuneCore Rising for releases; existing personal messaging/notes and a basic fan-store account add no selected subscription in this basket. Distribution/sample services are optional and external; this is not a claim the whole basket is local-only. [analysis; O01/O08/O09/O17 and T12]

```
Annual recurring software = (19.99 × 12) + 24.99
                          = 239.88 + 24.99
                          = USD 264.87
First-year software cash  = 264.87 + 60.00 one-time DAW licence
                          = USD 324.87
```

A creator keeping an already owned DAW does not incur that new60 purchase. Selecting Sounds+ at12.99 instead of Creator changes recurring cost to **155.88 +24.99 =180.87**. Using only already owned/free sounds or not distributing changes it again; those are alternative scenarios, not measured cancellations. [analysis; T06, O01, O09]

### B. Deliberately administration-heavy comparison

```
Splice Creator       19.99 ×12 = 239.88
TuneCore Rising                  24.99
Bandcamp Pro         10.00 ×12 = 120.00
Notion Plus          10.00 ×12 = 120.00
Workspace Starter     7.00 ×12 =  84.00
QuickBooks Start     38.00 ×12 = 456.00
Annual recurring total        = USD 1,044.87
Plus new REAPER licence        = USD 1,104.87 first year
```

This basket illustrates overbuying risk for a hobbyist; it is not a credible savings claim without the client's bills and need. Kora, Saltado, Airtable and alternative DAWs were **not** added again. Splice/TuneCore/Bandcamp services are not automatically displaced by a local companion. [analysis; O01/O08/O09/O10/O13/O15/O16]

**Excluded, not assumed zero:** tax/FX, transaction or revenue-share fees, storage media, backup destination, hardware, plugin upgrades, electricity, migration/review, support and opportunity cost. Actual cancellable spend, local operating cost and hours returned remain null until measured. The [existing SISO value model](https://github.com/sisodias/great-library-of-siso/blob/main/research/industries/source/intelligence/agency/economics/observed-value-model.json) requires that distinction.
