# 02 — Twelve stages and the records that join them

Date: **2026-09-13**. **12 stages.** Machine-readable equivalent: [02-workflow.json](02-workflow.json). This is a proposed operating model for the explicitly hypothetical one-site operator in [Stage 1](01-person.md), not an observation of AC's business. All triggers, frequencies, handoffs and data structures below are **[analysis]**. The commercial capability basis is the sourced [Stage 3 map](03-companies.md). Observed counts and durations are null until measured.

The flow is a service-day sequence with recurring loops, not a claim that every restaurant executes each step exactly once. Purchasing, safety checks, messages and exceptions can recur during service. The persistence design uses six aggregate families; it does not collapse restaurant work into six database tables or one authority domain.

## The chat surface is a work queue

[analysis] **Channels actually used by AC: unknown.** Inventory WhatsApp Business, Instagram DMs, SMS, email and phone before choosing adapters. The proposed owner is the host during staffed service and the owner-manager during preparation; assignment and after-hours cover must be accepted by the business. An example acceptance target is a ten-minute staffed-hours acknowledgement, **not a measured current response time or a promised SLA**. Measure p50/p95 response time and missed requests before choosing a target.

[analysis] Five proposed repeatable intents are opening/service hours, table availability or a booking change, menu availability and dietary/allergen questions, collection/delivery timing, and an existing order/refund query. These are hypotheses to classify against real messages. An allergen or safety question is routed to an authorised human using current approved information; an assistant must not improvise an assurance. Refund eligibility/amount remains an authorised decision.

[analysis] The conversion is explicit: `message → enquiry → identity/details confirmed → capacity/menu checked → human-approved reservation or order → acknowledgement to verified recipient`. Store the original message reference, extracted fields, reviewer, timestamp, authoritative-system ID and outbound-message ID. Use idempotency keys for repeated webhooks and edited messages. Replayed messages must not create another booking, charge, refund or kitchen ticket.

[analysis] Do not combine an enquiry with a reservation simply because they share a name and date. Distinguish draft, awaiting approval, accepted, rejected, cancelled and superseded records. The host confirms capacity; the POS/payment provider owns accepted payment state; the kitchen owns station acknowledgement. A dashboard can display those states without owning their authority.

## Six aggregate families, explicit boundaries

| Aggregate family | What persists | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Party | Scoped customer, supplier or employee identities and external-ID mappings | Role-specific schemas and access. No automatic identity merge on similar names; no cross-client people graph. Payroll details are not guest-service data. |
| ServiceCase | Linked enquiry, reservation, waitlist and order records with separate type/state machines | Conversion links preserve the source; they do not erase earlier records or make all case types interchangeable. |
| ProductDefinition | Supplier item, ingredient, menu item, modifier and versioned recipe relationships | Pack unit, recipe yield, effective cost and allergen source stay explicit. A recipe change does not rewrite historical sales. |
| StockMovement | Receipt, consumption, waste, count correction and transfer events | Keep quantity/unit/source and reversal lineage. Calculated availability is not proof of physical stock or food safety. |
| WorkRecord | Typed shift, prep, kitchen, check, exception and approval records | A common index does not imply shared permissions or legal authority. Domain records remain in their owning modules. |
| FinancialRecord | Invoice/credit, payment/refund, fee, payout, close and export/posting references | Preserve currencies, periods, gross/net distinctions and authority. Reversals, not silent deletion, correct accepted records. |

[analysis] The strongest joins are ServiceCase↔WorkRecord↔FinancialRecord for fulfilment, and ProductDefinition↔StockMovement↔FinancialRecord for food cost. HR and safety have fewer transactional joins and should remain bounded modules behind the same navigation and evidence index. **This is a coherent operations core with adjacent modules, not one universal restaurant database.** If real client work lacks these joins, a simpler integration suite is the better design.

## Measurement and falsification

[analysis] For each stage collect frequency, baseline human minutes, assisted minutes **including review/rework**, error types, unresolved exceptions, consent/authority failures and attributable cost. Keep `observed_frequency`, `baseline_minutes`, `assisted_minutes` and `actual_savings` null until there is evidence. Reject the wedge if the retained incumbent or a free configuration resolves the problem with less work, if review erases the time saving, or if a critical order/payment/privacy/safety error is introduced.
