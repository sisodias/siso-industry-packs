# Community owners — research checkpoint

**PARTIAL · 2026-09-14 · research and assembly only; no application code or deployment.**

[analysis] **Person/segment:** a founder and part-time moderator running an evergreen professional peer/accountability community. Explicit design scenario: 300 average paid members at $49/month, two staff and 10,000 permissioned email contacts. Not an interviewed customer or market average. Members buy ongoing useful relationships and facilitated progress; courses are secondary.

[analysis] **Wedge:** conversation → verified receipt → authorized membership → first useful peer connection. Test read-only reconciliation and human-approved resolution before asking every member to migrate.

[analysis] **Stack tax:** $4,800/year fixed + $1,764 modeled Circle fee = **$6,564/year**. Retain Workspace/QBO $624: maximum candidate displacement **$5,940**. Client VPS plus weekly system backup: $1,382.40/year; assumed maintenance adds $1,800. Difference is **$2,757.60 minus still-unpriced email/channel/license/recovery costs**, before migration. Initial migration allowance $6,000; **4.2h/week returned is unmeasured**, not a cash saving. See [priced basket](03-companies.md), [sources](evidence/commercial-sources.json) and [value model](06-value.md).

[analysis] **Spine:** Member · Offer · Membership · Interaction · Experience · LedgerEntry. Native community, inbox, processor and accountant retain their own record authority. The spine stores identity links, source versions, permissions and human decisions. One client-owned VPS; no SISO-hosted member data. [Proposed assembly](05-superapp.md).
