This is a discovery specification, not a recovered client file. [Workflow derivation](02-workflow.md).

[analysis] Airtable, Notion, Monday and ClickUp can carry a case/chase register; a checkbox cannot replace quality evidence. No attributable APE deployment of these tools was found. ## Ceiling/floor gap and company-first gate

[analysis] Major firms combine inventory ownership, supplier reach, repair/logistics, evidence and availability commitments—not merely software. Small operators can already buy capable RFQ/ERP automation, often alongside separate marketplaces and implementation costs. The accessible gap is **a client-owned case and evidence spine spanning conversations, offers, holds, shipments and core/credit follow-up**. OSS cannot manufacture inventory, market liquidity, OEM authority or staffed 24/7 coverage.

[analysis] The company-first research map contains **10 Tier-1 companies, 14 Tier-2 offerings, ten stages and ten complaint excerpts**, with priced inputs separated from unknowns. It preceded candidate searching. This records the evidence and missing quotes; it does not claim private interviews or complete per-tool complaint coverage. Stage 4 remains incomplete.
