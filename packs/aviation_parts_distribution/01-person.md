# 01 — The person: an independent AOG sourcing coordinator

Evidence observed **2026-09-13**. `[vendor]` includes operator and product publishers; `[practitioner]` is an attributed user account, not a verified causal measurement; `[analysis]` identifies the proposed operating model. This is research, not an APE interview or a compliance opinion.

## What is known, and what is constructed

[vendor] Aircraft Parts Europe describes UK-based sourcing for airlines, MROs, business aviation, helicopter operators and other buyers. Its AOG page asks for part number, aircraft type and airport, then describes source → quote → approval → shipping/document handoff. [APE about](https://www.aircraftpartseurope.com/pages/about-us) · [APE AOG process](https://www.aircraftpartseurope.com/pages/business-private-jet-aog-support-uk-europe-global).

[vendor] Its homepage contains catalogue claims of 80k and 100K+ parts; its public LinkedIn profile advertises 170,000+. These are inconsistent catalogue descriptions, **not independently verified owned-stock counts**. [Homepage](https://www.aircraftpartseurope.com/) · [Company profile](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/aircraft-parts-europe).

[analysis] The supplied lead brief says Shopify, 80–100k part numbers, an RFQ builder and New/Overhauled/Serviceable conditions. Treat Shopify and internal catalogue size as discovery inputs until a client-admin export confirms them. We have not inspected APE's private systems, employee roster, mailbox or invoices.

**One operator:** [analysis] design for the sales-and-sourcing coordinator at an APE-like independent distributor in the UK: an employee who owns an incoming RFQ through a deliverable quote and watches dispatch, but cannot personally approve every airworthiness, credit or export exception. Model a five-person company—owner/commercial approver, two sourcing/sales staff, stores/quality lead, and finance/admin—not an assertion about APE's headcount. The owner may cover several roles; preserve approval separation even then.

**What the customer buys:** [analysis] the correct, acceptable-condition component, with the required evidence, delivered to the agreed recipient before the operational deadline. A cheap quotation without stock confirmation, acceptable documentation or a viable shipping route does not fulfil that promise. This definition follows the AOG handoff described by APE; it is not a promise that the distributor approves installation. [AOG source](https://www.aircraftpartseurope.com/pages/business-private-jet-aog-support-uk-europe-global).

## Their Tuesday — a testable reconstruction, not a field observation

[analysis] Every time and workload below is an interview hypothesis. Baseline **50 customer RFQs/week**, five workdays, approximately 25 sent customer quotes/week; supplier replies and multi-line messages must be counted separately when buying software. Do not mistake catalogue cardinality for transaction volume.

| Time | What happens at this desk | Software / artifact | Waiting on / handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:00 | Accept overnight AOG handoff; identify airport, required-by time and who is actually answering. | Shared email, RFQ queue, call notes, supplier threads. | Previous shift and customer maintenance controller. |
| 09:00 | Turn a PDF or pasted list into exact part-number lines; ask about acceptable conditions and required release. | RFQ, original attachment, approved alternate evidence, catalogue. | Customer clarification; no guessed substitution. |
| 10:00 | Compare owned stock, approved suppliers and locator listings; request price, confirmed availability and certificates. | Supplier RFQs, listing snapshots and a comparison grid. | Supplier sales and their warehouse/quality staff. |
| 11:00 | Check serial/lot identity, certificate scope, issuer and provenance; isolate missing or inconsistent evidence. | Evidence dossier and quality hold. | Qualified reviewer and supplier, not an OCR confidence score. |
| 12:00 | Calculate landed cost, expiry, currency, core liability and margin; prepare a versioned customer quote. | Quote workbook/ERP and approval task. | Owner for unusual margin, credit or liability. |
| 13:00 | Customer accepts, but payment/credit and export questions may still block shipment. | PO, sales order, compliance decision and deposit record. | Finance, export adviser, customer purchasing. |
| 14:00 | Reserve or buy; stores checks the actual unit against the promised part and documentation. | Purchase order, receipt, serial/lot label, quarantine queue. | Supplier, receiving inspector and stores. |
| 15:00 | Arrange packaging, carrier cutoff and delivery to the named handler—not merely the airport postcode. | Packing list, shipment, airwaybill, courier/handler confirmation. | Forwarder, dangerous-goods specialist where relevant. |
| 16:00 | Update the customer; chase delivery proof, match supplier invoice, record FX and payment. | Conversation linked to shipment/order; accounting bridge. | Courier, bank and bookkeeper. |
| 17:00 | Chase overdue cores, RMAs and supplier credits; hand off open AOGs and pending holds. | Core/return register, audit trail, calendar reminders. | Next duty person, repairer and finance. |

## Workflow segments — choose by operational difference

[analysis] Segmentation is a proposed synthesis of APE's buyer scope and the service portfolios in [03-companies.md](03-companies.md), not a statistical market census.

| Segment | Distinct workflow | Design decision |
|---|---|---|
| Independent back-to-back AOG broker / distributor | Source from a network, align condition/cert/availability, price a time-critical delivery; limited owned inventory. | **Primary**, initially civil business/private aviation and routine replenishment. Closest to the lead and smallest credible wedge. |
| Stockholding rotable/exchange distributor | Serialised stock, exchange fees, core deadlines, repair disposition and credits. | Share the spine; add core lifecycle before promising exchanges. |
| OEM-franchised consumables supplier | Replenishment, lot/shelf-life controls, contract prices, high line counts. | Later preset; optimise batch/lot handling, not aircraft telemetry. |
| Teardown / USM / life-limited-part trader | Asset provenance, serial history, removal evidence, times/cycles and life accounting. | Higher evidence burden; excluded from automatic eligibility decisions. |
| Pooling / PBH provider | Fleet commitments, exchange availability and contract-level risk across stock pools. | Ceiling reference; not a first-client service promise. |
| Repair broker | Repair orders, shop quotes/approvals, turnaround promises and return-to-stock. | Adjacent module; do not silently become a certified MRO system. |
| General-aviation catalogue merchant | Frequent lower-value sales, direct checkout, fitment questions and consumer-style support. | Separate pricing/checkout preset; Q01 warns marketplace coverage may differ. |
| Defence / controlled-goods distributor | Programme authorisations, classification, end-use/end-user and jurisdiction-specific controls. | Not the initial segment; no bypass for urgency. |
